# Logo Sources & Attribution (Arsenal Section)

This document lists the provenance and official source URLs for each tool logo in `/public/logos/`.
In accordance with portfolio guidelines:
- Official brand assets or press kits are used in their unmodified original brand colours.
- For Simple Icons (`siCoreldraw`, `siDavinciresolve`), single-colour glyphs are rendered in white on a rounded square filled with the brand's official hex colour (Rule 2b).
- No logos have been redrawn, recoloured, distorted, or invented.
- All files are verified local SVGs or PNGs under 30 KB.

## Missing Logos & Manual Supply Notice
- **OpenAI Codex (`codex.svg`)**: OpenAI does not maintain a standalone trademark or separate official logo for Codex (it has been sunsetted as an independent product and unified into ChatGPT). Per Rule 2 ("Never redraw, recolour, distort or invent a logo"), no fabricated logo has been created. The ARSENAL section gracefully displays the bold initial fallback tile (`C`) on the mint column strip color. If you wish to use a custom or legacy logo, place `codex.svg` directly into `/public/logos/`.
